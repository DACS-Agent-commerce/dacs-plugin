            Help and recovery

            What is simulated?

            Everything. The chain, directory and proof publisher are local fixtures started by the offline launcher. Fixture DEM has no value; a receipt is a fixture chain observation, not settlement. Nothing is sent to Demos testnet, GitHub, X or ChatGPT.

            Why two wallets and two windows?

            Use a throwaway buyer wallet. The fixture supplies the seller and its Listing.

            I reloaded, closed a tab or got interrupted

            Reloading this page is safe: the state lives in the launcher and nothing is repeated. The "Unfinished steps" box lists anything started but not finished. Reopen the portal link shown in that step to continue, or cancel it. Pressing Prepare again returns the same pending purchase or listing instead of creating a second one. A wallet approval you closed or rejected was not sent.

            The launcher was restarted

            Every start is a fresh run with new fixtures. A red notice appears and all earlier dashboard state is gone. Sign in again; the wallets in your browser still exist.

            Cancelling and declining

            Press Reject (or Escape) in the wallet dialog, or close the portal tab, to decline. Pressing Lock in the wallet while a request is open cancels it; nothing is signed or sent. A pending purchase or an unpublished listing can be cancelled here; a cancelled one never shows a receipt or a published stage.

            Keyboard use

            Tab and Shift+Tab move between controls; Enter or Space presses buttons and Enter submits the listing and search forms. The "Skip to steps" link and the step list above jump to each section. After a problem, focus moves to the message that explains it; for the listing form, to the first field to fix.

            Common messages

            "Sign in first": finish the matching sign-in. "Find and verify the test service first": verify a listing in step 3. "The buyer wallet is the seller wallet": sign in as the buyer from the other window. "still being published" or "still awaiting_approval": finish or cancel the pending one first.
